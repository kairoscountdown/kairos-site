Common questions

Subscriptions

Restore a purchase. Open Kairos, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Manage or cancel. Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Kairos.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Widgets

If a widget looks out of date, touch and hold it, choose Edit Widget to confirm its settings, or remove and re-add it from the widget gallery. Widgets refresh on a schedule set by iOS, so a brief delay after a change is normal.
